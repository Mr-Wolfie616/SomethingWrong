This is a Game Jam made with the prompt Something Wrong
It was made by a team of 11 people all from the University of Winchesters Immersive Technologies Course. 2 Producers, 3 Game developers, 4 Artists, 1 Level Designer and 1 Sound Designer.
Producers: Ryan & Kian
Game Developers: Dan, Jack & Avery
Artists: Louis, Josh, Caitlin & Solar
Level Designer: Bradley
Sound Designer: Zoey
The controls are W,A,S,D & Left Click is attack


HAMSTERS!!! Embrace as a hampocolypse takes over your cage all the hamsters have gone mental. Fight your way through tunnels whilst fending
off the Minature Rogue Hammies as they battle you in gladiator style tube arenas. BUT beware don't let them beat you up too much as they may cause you
difficulty moving.
